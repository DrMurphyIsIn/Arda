## 0. What this is for

The falsification zoo (`examples/quasicrystal/zoo.py`, `check_multiplicativity`) decides
the (B-mult) / (B-mult-twisted) clause of QC_AXIOMS_DRAFT.md (appendices v2 W3a, v3 W3c,
v4 A1b, v5 Dedekind) by ONE finite computation: from the Dirichlet coefficients `a(n)` of
an object it builds the coefficients `b(n)` of `-F'/F` by the divisor recursion

    a(n) log n = sum_{d | n} b(d) a(n/d)          (n >= 1, a(1) = 1),

and then reads two things off `b`:

1. **the composite test** -- does `b(n)` vanish at every `n` that is not a prime power
   (first instance `n = 6`)?
2. **the generation law** -- on the prime layer, is `b(p^m) = (log p) t(p)^m` for a single
   scalar `t(p)` per prime?

Until now the zoo's docstring ASSERTED what these two readings certify.  Lemma O PROVES
it, in Mathlib `ArithmeticFunction` form:

    (O)   b supported on prime powers   <=>   a is multiplicative on coprime pairs
                                              (Mathlib `ArithmeticFunction.IsMultiplicative`)
    (O')  for multiplicative a:
          b(p^k) = (log p) a(p)^k for all p, k >= 1   <=>   a is COMPLETELY multiplicative

So a composite leak `b(6) != 0` is a kernel-certified refutation of multiplicativity (the
DH and Epstein verdicts), and a prime layer that is not geometric in `a(p)` is a
kernel-certified refutation of COMPLETE multiplicativity even when `b(6) = 0` (the GL(2)
Satake verdict of `SatakeDegreeTwo.lean`, whose `scalarGenerated_powerSum_iff` says the
layer `(log p)(alpha^m + beta^m)` is geometric iff `alpha beta = 0`).

## 1. The statement, precisely

Fix a commutative ring `R`, an amplitude `a : ArithmeticFunction R` with `a 1 = 1`, and a
**completely additive weight** `l : N -> R` (`l (m n) = l m + l n` for `m, n >= 1`; the
intended instance is `l n = Real.log n`).  The **log-derivative coefficient functional** is

    IsLogDerivCoeff l a b  :=  forall n >= 1,  a n * l n = (b * a) n

with `*` Mathlib's Dirichlet convolution, i.e. `sum_{d | n} b d * a (n / d)`
(`mul_apply_divisors`).

| theorem (`LemmaO.` namespace) | hypotheses | statement |
|---|---|---|
| `logDerivCoeff_unique` | `a 1 = 1` | two solutions of the recursion are equal |
| `logDerivCoeffOf_spec` / `exists_logDerivCoeff` | `a 1 = 1` | a solution exists (strong recursion) |
| `primePow_support_of_isMultiplicative` | `l` completely additive, `a` multiplicative | `b n = 0` for every non-prime-power `n` |
| `isMultiplicative_of_primePow_support` | + `IsDomain R`, `l n != 0` for `n >= 2` | prime-power support => `a` multiplicative |
| `lemmaO` | as above | **the iff (O)** |
| `not_isMultiplicative_of_composite_leak` | `l` completely additive | one composite `b n != 0` refutes multiplicativity |
| `generation_law_of_completelyMultiplicative` | `l` completely additive, `a` completely multiplicative | `b (p^k) = l p * a p ^ k` |
| `completelyMultiplicative_of_generation_law` | + `IsDomain R`, `l n != 0`, `a` multiplicative | generation law => completely multiplicative |
| `generation_law_iff_completelyMultiplicative` | as above | **the iff (O')** |
| `not_completelyMultiplicative_of_generation_law_fails` | `l` completely additive | one prime power off the law refutes complete multiplicativity |
| `lemmaO_real`, `lemmaO_complex` | `a 1 = 1` | (O) at `l = log` over `R` and `C` |
| `generation_law_iff_completelyMultiplicative_real` / `_complex` | `a` multiplicative | (O') at `l = log` |

**Why the weight hypotheses are there.**  The backward direction of (O) and the forward
direction of (O') hold over ANY commutative ring with no condition on `l` beyond additivity.
The other two directions must CANCEL `l`: the recursion at a coprime product `m k` yields
`a(mk) l(mk) = a(m) a(k) l(mk)`, and only `l(mk) != 0` in a domain turns that into
`a(mk) = a(m) a(k)`.  This is not bookkeeping: with `l = 0` the recursion is solved by
`b = 0` for EVERY `a`, so "prime-power support" would be vacuous.  `Real.log` is
non-vanishing at every `n >= 2` (`logWeight_ne_zero`), which is why the zoo's reading is
sound.

**Proof shape.**  Both directions of (O) are strong inductions driven by one lemma,
`sum_divisors_coprime_split`: for coprime `m, k >= 2` and a summand vanishing at `1` and at
every proper non-prime-power divisor of `m k`, the divisor sum of `m k` collapses to the
`d = m k` term plus the divisor sums of `m` and of `k` -- because a prime-power divisor of a
coprime product divides one factor (`Nat.Coprime.isPrimePow_dvd_mul`).  The non-prime-power
`n` is split as `ordProj[p] n * ordCompl[p] n` at `p = n.minFac`
(`exists_coprime_factorization`).  (O') is a strong induction on the exponent through the
prime-power recursion `Nat.sum_divisors_prime_pow`, and its converse finishes with
`IsMultiplicative.multiplicative_factorization` + `Finsupp.prod_add_index'`.

## 2. The witnesses at n = 6 (`LemmaOWitnesses.lean`)

`6 = 2 * 3` is the first composite integer, so `b 6` is the first place a
non-multiplicative amplitude can leak.  `b_six` gives the closed form for ANY real
amplitude with `a 1 = 1`:

    b 6 = a 6 log 6 - a 2 a 3 (log 2 + log 3).

| object | `a 2, a 3, a 6` | `b 6` | Lemma O verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| zeta (`ζ`, positive control) | `1, 1, 1` | `0` (`zeta_b_six`, from multiplicativity, not from `Λ`'s definition) | multiplicative; functional IS `Λ` (`zeta_functional_eq_vonMangoldt`, via `vonMangoldt_mul_zeta`); generation law `Λ (p^k) = log p` (`zeta_generation_law`) |
| Davenport-Heilbronn (`dhAmp`, period 5, `[1, κ, -κ, -1, 0]`, `κ = Quasicrystal.dhKappa`) | `κ, -κ, 1` | `(1 + κ²) log 6 > 0` (`dh_b_six`, `dh_b_six_pos`; numerically `1.9364`, the leakage node's enclosed value) | **NOT multiplicative** (`dhAmp_not_isMultiplicative_uncond`) |
| Epstein `E` for `x² + 5y²` (`epsteinAmp`, `a n = r(n)/2`) | `0, 0, 2` | `2 log 6 != 0` (`epstein_b_six`) | **NOT multiplicative** (`epsteinAmp_not_isMultiplicative_uncond`); direct cross-check `a 6 = 2 != 0 = a 2 a 3` (`_direct`) |

The Epstein representation numbers are COUNTED in the kernel by `decide`
(`rep5_one = 2`, `rep5_two = 0`, `rep5_three = 0`, `rep5_six = 4`) over the lattice box
`|x|, |y| <= n`, and the box is proved complete and sound (`rep5_box_complete`,
`rep5Set_sound`, `rep5_eq_card`): `rep5 n` is the true `#{(x, y) in Z² : x² + 5y² = n}`,
not a truncation.  No `native_decide`.

**Why `E` matters -- the counterfeit-ladder object.**  `E(s) = (1/2) sum' (x² + 5y²)^{-s}`
is the Epstein zeta of the principal form of discriminant `-20`.  It has the same
functional-equation profile as `zeta_K` for `Q(sqrt(-5))` (it is the half-sum of `zeta_K`
and the genus-character L-function), so every SUPPORT-side clause of the axiom list sees it
as a legitimate arithmetic comb.  But it has no Euler product, and Lemma O detects that at
the very first composite frequency: `a 2 = a 3 = 0` while `a 6 = 2`.  It is the object the
multiplicativity clause exists to reject, and now the rejection is a theorem rather than a
floating-point verdict.  (Note the normalization: `a 1 = r(1)/2 = 1` forces `a 6 = 2`, since
`x² + 5y² = 6` has the four solutions `(±1, ±1)`; any nonzero value leaks.)

## 3. Which zoo check this justifies, exactly

`check_multiplicativity` (zoo.py) computes `b` by `_von_mangoldt_analogue` -- the same
recursion as `IsLogDerivCoeff` with `l = log`, over complex coefficients -- and

* FAILs an object on `|b(n)| > tol` at a composite `n <= N`: by `lemmaO_complex`
  (backward direction, `not_isMultiplicative_of_composite_leak`) that object's amplitude
  is not multiplicative, hence has no Euler product of any degree;
* FAILs an object on `b(p^m) != (log p) t(p)^m`: by
  `not_completelyMultiplicative_of_generation_law_fails` (with `t(p) = a(p)`) that
  amplitude is not COMPLETELY multiplicative, i.e. not a degree-1 Euler product -- which is
  exactly the A1b/Dedekind finding that the clause is a degree-1 predicate;
* PASSes zeta and `L(s, chi)`: by `generation_law_of_completelyMultiplicative`.

What the zoo does that the kernel does NOT: the floating-point tolerance `tol = 1e-6` and
the truncation at `N = 60`.  Lemma O is exact and unbounded in `n`; the zoo is a numerical
instance of it.  The zoo's docstring now points to `LemmaO.lemmaO_real` (one line; no
behaviour change).
