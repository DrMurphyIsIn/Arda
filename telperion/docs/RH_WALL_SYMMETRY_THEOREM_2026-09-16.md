## The deep result: reflection invariance is FORCED (for the symmetric class)
All four sweeps hit the SAME wall from disjoint directions. The unifying cause is now
a **theorem** on a precise class, not a coincidence:

> **FORCED (kernel-formalizable).** Any functional built solely from the completed zeta
> `Ξ` (evenness `Ξ(1−s)=Ξ(s)`) together with complex conjugation — and any de
> Bruijn–Newman **flow-robust** certificate — is invariant under `ρ ↦ 1−conj(ρ)`.
> *Proof shape:* `Ξ`'s zero set is closed under the **Klein four-group**
> `{1, s↦1−s, s↦conj s, s↦1−conj s}`; a functional expressed through `Ξ` and its
> conjugate is symmetric on each orbit, hence **constant on every reflection pair**
> `{ρ, 1−conj ρ}` (same ordinate, mirrored real parts `{β, 1−β}`). Flow-robustness forces
> it too: the only flow statement that breaks reflection is `Λ<0`, which contradicts
> Rodgers–Tao `Λ≥0` (unconditional, arXiv:1801.05914).

Such a functional therefore **cannot separate an on-line zero from an off-line pair** —
so it cannot force `[Re ρ = ½]`. That is the wall, as a symmetry theorem.

## The escape, precisely located
The **Euler product / primes do not respect `s↦1−conj s`** (`Σ n^{−s}` is manifestly
non-symmetric). So reflection-breaking unconditional objects DO exist — `ξ'` (odd under the
reflection), Guth–Maynard `N(σ,T)`, Levinson/PRZZ positive-proportion, one-level density at
complex argument. The wall is no longer "no joint object exists." It is located **one step
later**: every such object loses its reflection-breaking bite **exactly at the per-zero
forcing step** — it is *capped* (Levinson ≤ 5/12), *aggregate* (density: few ≠ none), or
*super-RH* (`Λ<0`). The arithmetic enters only **non-robustly** (averaged / exceptional-set),
never per-zero — the de Bruijn–Newman marginality no-go.

## The one genuinely open escape hatch
**Non-self-dual `GL(n)` families** (unitary type, complex root numbers): the conjugation
symmetry does NOT close the quadruple, so a reflection-breaking one-level-density functional
might retain per-zero bite. **Flagged, not refuted, not asserted to work** — the single
undischarged direction the campaign could not close.
