## The single genuine foothold (horizontal)
**PRZZ >5/12** (arXiv:1802.10521): unconditional, partial, non-trivial, and it does *real*
reality-forcing — it certifies `Re=½` for a positive **density of individual zeros**
(Levinson κ(½)~⅓ 1974 → Conrey κ(4/7)>2/5 1989 → PRZZ >5/12 2018). But it is a **landmark,
not a lever**: its own route to 100% is RH-equivalent (Bettin-Gonek arXiv:1604.02740 —
finite mollifier length θ certifies only `Re > ½ + 1/(2θ)`; proportion→1 iff θ→∞ iff RH).

## The unifying cause (the real prize)
Every unconditional single-coordinate functional is **invariant under the functional-
equation reflection `ρ ↦ 1 − conj(ρ)`**, so it *cannot separate an on-line zero from an
off-line symmetric pair at the same height* — hence cannot force the discrete predicate
`[Re ρ = ½]` without an RH-strength input. This is the one obstruction behind all three
axes, and it is now **kernel-certified from every side**:
- weight: `not_completelyMultiplicative_vonMangoldt` (multiplicativity is free);

- horizontal density: the same reflection (density counts one member of each pair).
